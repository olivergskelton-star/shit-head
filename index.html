The house rules

How to play Shit Head

Get rid of every card. First player out wins. The last player left holding cards is the Shit Head.

  1. 1

    Choose your seat and drink

    Use VIEW AS at the top of the screen to select Oliver, Dan or Chris. Your hand moves to the bottom. Tap your name on the paper notepad to change this week’s display name, and tap your drink/coaster during setup to choose Wine, Beer, Coffee, Tea, JD & Coke or G&T.

  2. 2

    Set your table

    You start with 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up table cards and 4 cards in your hand. Tap a hand card, then a face-up card to swap them. Repeat as needed, use SORT if helpful, then press READY.

  3. 3

    Start low

    The player holding the lowest eligible card in their final hand starts. Opening order begins 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, J, Q, K, then 10. A, 2 and 3 are treated as high/special for the opening check and do not qualify. READY order breaks a tie.

  4. 4

    Select and play

    The red glow shows whose turn it is. Tap a card so it lifts, then press PLAY. You may select several cards if they have the same rank. Play equal to or higher than the live card on the pile.

  5. 5

    Draw or pick up

    While the draw pile remains, refill your hand to at least 3 cards. If you cannot play—or choose not to—press PICK UP to take the whole pile. If another matching card is offered, add it or press FINISH TURN.

  6. 6

    Finish the table

    Once the draw pile is gone, finish your hand. If your final hand cards match a visible table card, you may include it in the same play. The three table positions then act independently: play any visible top card, or choose any exposed face-down card blind as soon as the card above it has gone. Players with no cards left are OUT and are skipped.

Special cards

2

Reset
Play it on anything. The next player may play anything.

3

Transparent
Play it on anything. The last non-3 card underneath stays live.

7

Low
The next player must play a 7 or lower.

10

Burn
Clear the pile into the burn stack and play again.

4×

Four of a kind burns
Four matching ranks in a row clear the pile. Three 8s also burn it.
